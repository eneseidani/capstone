Metric interpretation
- ROC-AUC: ranking ability; 0.50 is random ranking.
- Average precision: precision-recall summary; compare it with the test positive-class rate.
- Balanced accuracy: average of recall and specificity; useful for class imbalance.
- Precision: fraction of selected observations that were actually positive.
- Recall: fraction of all actual positive observations selected by the model.
- Specificity: fraction of actual negative observations correctly rejected.
- Brier score and log loss: probability quality; lower is better.
- Coverage: fraction of observations passing the fixed probability threshold.
- Selection lift: selected precision divided by the overall positive-class rate; 1.0 means no precision improvement over the base rate.
- TN, FP, FN, TP: confusion-matrix counts.

Comparison cautions
- Dummy is a baseline, not a competitive model.
- Threshold sensitivity is descriptive only; do not choose a threshold by optimizing test results.
- Per-year and per-ticker metrics can be noisy, especially for small samples.
- Feature importance/coefficient outputs are model-specific diagnostics, not causal effects; do not compare raw magnitudes across methods.
- A better classifier is not automatically a better portfolio. S06 must assess diversification, turnover, transaction costs and net portfolio returns.
- The pre-2023 training labels that mature on or after 2023-01-01 have been removed; the 2023+ test sample is unchanged.
- Where S03 does not provide Label_End_Date, dates are reconstructed from available per-asset records. This can be conservative if some asset dates were dropped during feature construction/merging.
